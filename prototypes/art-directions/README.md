# WWW-001 — Internal Art-Direction Prototypes

Status: **Internal visual exploration — not a production selection**  
Date: 2026-09-27

These prototypes advance WWW-001 while direct farmer access is unavailable.

They use:
- the same provisional H3 v2 message;
- the same statistics-calibrated synthetic Field Profitability scenario;
- the same evidence/confidence labels;
- the same CTA intent;
- the same current product boundary.

They intentionally **do not** share one layout/DOM scaffold.

## Directions

### A — Evidence-Led Editorial
Origin:
economic evidence, definitions, restraint, inspectable detail.

Primary risk:
can look like a report, consultancy or financial publication rather than an agricultural product company.

### B — Farm Operations Layer
Origin:
real production context, operational records and the transition from farm activity to economics.

Primary risk:
can be misclassified as mapping, telemetry or precision-ag software.

### C — Economic Control Room
Origin:
decision state, economics, downside/upside visibility and operational seriousness.

Primary risk:
can look like ERP/accounting software or overstate current product maturity.

## Photography constraint

No generated/synthetic image is presented as documentary agriculture.

Until an approved D8 image exists, prototypes use an explicit **documentary asset placeholder**. This preserves composition space without faking provenance.

The placeholder is a limitation of the current visual-fidelity round.

## Transfer diagnostic

Each direction contains a small internal master-brand transfer diagnostic across:
- crop;
- horticulture / greenhouse;
- pig production;
- dairy / other livestock.

These are **not product modules** and contain no fabricated domain metrics. They test whether the visual grammar survives when the production unit changes.

## Evaluation

Use the art-direction protocol:
`docs/experiments/art-direction-farmer-test-v1.md`

Before human testing, internal red-team questions:
- Does the concept explain economics faster than it advertises technology?
- Does it still feel agricultural without generic green/field clichés?
- Does it survive logo-off?
- Does it survive without field geometry?
- Does evidence/confidence remain visible?
- Does it look more shipped or more certain than PROFIT really is?
- Can the same grammar transfer to orchard/greenhouse/pig/dairy contexts?
- Is the strongest memorable device actually distinctive, or just fashionable?

No direction is a winner until evidence supports that decision.
