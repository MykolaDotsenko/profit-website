# PROFIT VEV Standard v1

Status: **Approved internal product/economic methodology**
Date: **2026-09-27**
Owner: **Product / Farm Economics**
Applies to: public evidence labels, future pilot value assessments and the definition of Verified Economic Value (VEV).

## 1. Purpose

Verified Economic Value is an **evidence status for an incremental economic effect**, not a marketing adjective and not a promise that the effect is positive.

A VEV assessment may be positive, zero or negative.

The question is not whether the farmer had a good outcome. The question is what incremental economic effect can be supported by the evidence, relative to a defensible counterfactual, and how confidently that effect can be attributed.

## 2. Core record

A value assessment should preserve, where applicable:
1. customer / eligible production unit;
2. period;
3. baseline;
4. counterfactual;
5. PROFIT-supported intervention or decision;
6. actual outcome;
7. incremental economic effect;
8. attribution method;
9. confidence;
10. provenance / evidence sources;
11. calculation definition and assumptions;
12. reviewer / review record.

No single field above proves causality on its own.

## 3. Evidence ladder

### Hypothetical
- an illustration, scenario or example;
- not a realised customer outcome;
- may use assumptions, synthetic data or calibrated reference values;
- must never be presented as customer value.

Public wording: **An illustration or scenario. It is not a realised farm outcome.**

### Modelled
- produced from explicit assumptions, a deterministic model or predictive model;
- source inputs and assumptions are traceable;
- the effect has not yet been established as an actual realised farm outcome.

Public wording: **Calculated from explicit assumptions or a model. It is not yet a measured realised outcome.**

A modelled value is not VEV.

### Observed
- an actual outcome was measured on a real farm / production unit for a stated period;
- provenance is known;
- the economic calculation is reproducible;
- no causal attribution to PROFIT or a specific decision is implied.

Public wording: **A real outcome measured for a stated period. Observation alone does not show what caused it.**

Observed improvement is not VEV.

### Attributed
All Observed requirements, plus:
- a defined intervention / decision;
- a defensible baseline and counterfactual;
- an incremental economic effect;
- a stated attribution method;
- assessed confidence of **High, Medium or Low**;
- material assumptions and limitations are visible.

Public wording: **A measured incremental effect linked to a defined decision or intervention against an explicit counterfactual, with the attribution method and confidence stated.**

Not assessed and Insufficient evidence cannot support an Attributed label.

### Verified
All Attributed requirements, plus:
- economic definitions and calculations are reproducible;
- source/provenance records are sufficient for review;
- period and eligible customer / production-unit scope are explicit;
- no material unresolved contradiction exists in the evidence package;
- assessed confidence is **High or Medium**;
- a documented verification review confirms that the PROFIT VEV Standard has been met.

Public wording: **An attributed incremental economic effect that passes the PROFIT VEV verification standard: reproducible economics, documented evidence and counterfactual, assessed confidence, and recorded review.**

Verification concerns evidence quality. It does not guarantee that the value is positive.

## 4. Confidence is separate from evidence state
- **High** — evidence is strong, consistent, traceable and materially representative for the assessment.
- **Medium** — evidence is sufficient for the assessment but has material, disclosed limitations.
- **Low** — evidence supports a cautious assessment, but important limitations remain.
- **Insufficient evidence** — evidence is not sufficient to support the proposed assessment.
- **Not assessed** — confidence was not evaluated; this is a meta-state, not a confidence level.

Rules:
- Not assessed cannot support Attributed or Verified.
- Insufficient evidence cannot support Attributed or Verified.
- Low may support Attributed but not Verified.
- Medium or High may support Verified if every other verification condition passes.

## 5. Economic-value rule
Incremental economic effect must be expressed using an explicit economic definition appropriate to the production unit and decision.

Where relevant, PROFIT may report:
- VEV per customer;
- VEV per hectare or other production unit;
- VEV per € paid to PROFIT;
- share of eligible customers with positive VEV;
- attribution confidence;
- stated period and cohort.

Do not normalize livestock into per-hectare metrics unless that unit is genuinely relevant.

## 6. Counterfactual rule
A counterfactual must be stated, not implied.

Acceptable methods depend on context and may include:
- credible current-practice / do-nothing comparison;
- before/after with explicit adjustment and limitations;
- matched comparison;
- randomized or quasi-experimental design;
- validated causal/economic model where justified.

Method choice must be proportional to stakes, available data and cost of being wrong. A precise number with a weak counterfactual is not Verified.

## 7. PROFIT attribution rule
Do not attribute a farm improvement to PROFIT merely because PROFIT was used, the outcome improved afterwards, a forecast was correct, a model predicted avoided loss, or the farmer reports that the tool was useful.

Attribution must connect: **Baseline → Counterfactual → PROFIT-supported intervention → Actual outcome → Incremental effect → Attribution → Confidence.**

## 8. Review rule
A Verified assessment must include a review record containing reviewer identity or approved reviewer role, review date, assessment period, evidence package reference, conclusion and material limitations.

For high-stakes claims, independent or cross-functional review is preferred.

## 9. Public-site consequence
At approval of v1:
- no current website example becomes Observed, Attributed or Verified;
- statistics-calibrated Field 31 remains **Hypothetical · Confidence: Not assessed**;
- no customer/pilot result is published;
- future claims must satisfy the standard before promotion.

## 10. Reconsider if
Update this standard if real pilot evidence exposes a material flaw in the counterfactual rules, confidence thresholds, production-unit normalization, attribution requirements, reviewer independence or cohort/period handling.

Do not weaken the standard merely to increase the amount of Verified value reported.
