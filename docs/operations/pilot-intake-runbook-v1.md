# PROFIT Pilot Intake Runbook v1

Status: **Approved internal operating process**
Date: **2026-09-27**
Primary owner: **Mykola Dotsenko**

## Purpose

Define what happens after a prospective farmer submits the five-field pilot enquiry.

## Intake policy

- The public form requests only: name, farm/company, country, email and farm type.
- No farm records, production economics, machinery data, livestock records or files are requested at first contact.
- The reply channel is email to the address supplied by the enquirer.
- The target response time is **within two business days**.
- The first reply is for fit and context, not for collecting a dataset.

## Primary owner

Mykola Dotsenko is the primary intake owner for the current pre-launch / pilot stage.

The owner may delegate a conversation to another PROFIT team member when domain expertise is useful, but responsibility for ensuring the enquiry receives a response remains with the primary intake owner.

## First-response sequence

1. Confirm receipt and the purpose of the conversation.
2. Check whether the farm is a plausible fit for the current Field Profitability pilot.
3. If relevant, schedule a first conversation.
4. Do not request farm records until the purpose, scope and applicable privacy/data terms are established.
5. If the enquiry is not a current fit, say so directly and avoid collecting unnecessary information.

## Response target

Operational target: **reply within two business days**.

This is a service target, not an uptime/SLA guarantee. If the team cannot maintain it consistently, update the public copy and this runbook together rather than silently missing the promise.

## Fit logic

Best current fit:
- arable / field-crop farms where field- and season-level yield, price and cost records can support Field Profitability;
- farmers willing to discuss current decision problems and record availability.

Interest from horticulture, greenhouse and livestock farms may be recorded as master-brand discovery interest, but must not be represented as access to a current shipped module.

## Data boundary

Before any farm records are shared:
- the purpose must be explicit;
- the relevant privacy notice must be active;
- farm-data terms must be agreed where applicable;
- the farmer must understand what data is requested and why.

## Escalation

- Privacy/data-rights question → route to the approved privacy contact once configured.
- Investor/partner enquiry → route through the confirmed direct-contact path once configured.
- Product fit uncertainty → primary owner decides whether to involve the relevant domain team member.

## Release relationship

This runbook is sufficient evidence for the **pilot-process** release gate because owner, channel, timing and first-response behavior are now internally decided.

It is **not** evidence for:
- privacy-notice;
- data-terms;
- company-details;
- direct-contact;
- production form delivery.

The pilot endpoint remains technically blocked until the required form gates are READY.
